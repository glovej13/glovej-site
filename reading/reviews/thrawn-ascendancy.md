## Thrawn Ascendancy
Timothy Zahn — Science Fiction / Star Wars

- *Chaos Rising* (2020)
- *Greater Good* (2021)
- *Lesser Evil* (2021)

Zahn created Grand Admiral Thrawn back in 1991, and this trilogy is his return to the character's roots — a prequel set entirely within the Chiss Ascendancy, decades before Thrawn ever wears an Imperial uniform. Reception has generally been strong, and reviewers tend to land on the same conclusion: this is less a war story than a court-politics story wearing Star Wars trim, a deliberate shift from the earlier Thrawn books that most credit as a strength.

That shift is exactly why it worked for me. Star Wars has a habit of giving its villains origins that don't look much like villainy, and Thrawn is no exception — watching that origin take shape was the appeal of picking this trilogy up in the first place. But the part that actually held my attention wasn't Thrawn. It was the Chiss political system built around him: the Nine Ruling Families, the internal hierarchy, factions and alliances that have nothing to do with the Empire yet. That's the layered world-building the reviewers are pointing at, and it's the reason the read was worth it.
