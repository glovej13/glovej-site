## Breaking the Dark
Lisa Jewell — Crime Fiction / Marvel

Breaking the Dark launched Marvel's Crime line in 2024 — original adult crime novels built around street-level Marvel characters, with Jessica Jones going first (Luke Cage and Daredevil followed, written by S.A. Cosby and Alex Segura). Jewell, better known for domestic thrillers, wrote an original story rather than an adaptation: a mother hires Jones to investigate her teenage twins after they return from a UK visit unnervingly "perfected." Reception was generally positive, with reviewers crediting Jewell for nailing Jones's voice and for leaning the book toward crime fiction with a supernatural edge rather than a conventional superhero story.

I liked the Jessica Jones show when it came out — the whole Defenders corner of the Marvel Netflix universe was strong, and JJ was my favourite of the bunch.

I'm drawn to the less-powered end of the superhero spectrum. Jessica has abilities, but they're not overwhelming, and this book leans into that — it reads as a crime novel first, superhero story second, which lines up with why I liked the show to begin with. That's exactly the register I like my heroes in.
