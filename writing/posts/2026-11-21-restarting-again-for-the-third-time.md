So what have I been up to?

First, the management system I was building. It stalled, and I'll get to why in a second. I finally came back to it and really picked it up, and it's essentially done. Nothing's ever done in this space and I'll be tweaking it forever, but I've got a nice program set up that runs my to-dos, my tasks, my budget, my day-to-day activities and the publishing pipeline for some of my projects.

It helps me stay organized, and it runs on its own platform, so I'm no longer paying for other systems, which is what I'd been doing before.

The other big piece of work, and I don't think I ever posted about it here, is the Keep It Local website. It's been live for a couple of months now. Building it took all my focus, along with getting used to the maintenance and setting up the social media structure, and that's when the management system went on hold. It came out of the current politics and the push to buy Canadian and do what we can. So I built a site to review and score retail outlets. Not products, just the retail stores you go to every day. Who owns them? I trace the ownership and the operating structure to understand how much of each dollar spent in the store, from a store profit perspective, stays within the Canadian economy.

There are plenty of tools for looking at products. Honestly, that's really challenging to do, but some sites do it very well, with tens of thousands of products. I was never going to catch up to them. Nothing seemed to cover retailers, though, so that's the space I took.

Keep It Local has its own social account, and I've been posting there daily. That will slow down now that the site is up and running. If you haven't seen it yet, check it out at https://keepitlocaleh.ca. Please share the posts, because I'm trying to get it seen more widely. It's harder than I thought to get noticed on the social side of the Internet.
