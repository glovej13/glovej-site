There's the management system I was building. It kind of stopped, and I'll get to why in a second. I finally came back to it and really picked it up. It's essentially done. It helps me stay organized, and it's all standalone on its own platform instead of paying for other systems, which is what I'd been doing before.

The other big piece of work, and I don't think I ever posted about it here, is the Keep It Local website. It's been live for a couple of months now, and I really focused on that, which is when the management system went on hold. It's driven by the current politics of the time and the view of trying to buy Canadian and do what we can. So I built a site to review and score retail outlets. Not products, just the retail stores you go to every day. Who owns them? Trace the ownership, trace the operating structure, and understand how much of the dollar spent in that store, from a store profit perspective, stays within the Canadian economy.

There are plenty of tools for looking at products. Honestly, that's really challenging to do, but some sites do it very well with tens of thousands of products. I was never going to catch up or compete with them. Nothing seemed to sit in the space around retailers, though, so that's what I've done.

Keep It Local has its own account, and I've been posting there daily. That will cut down now that it's live and operating. If you haven't seen it yet, check it out at https://keepitlocaleh.ca. Please share the posts, because I'm trying to get it seen more widely. It's harder than I thought to get things out on the social side of the Internet.
